# Image credits

The DAAIF home-page rotation shares selected Swiss city photographs with the DaCa proof of
concept. The responsive AVIF and WebP files are hosted locally. Their authentic geography and
landmark composition are retained; processing is documented per asset.

| Asset | Source | Author | Licence | Processing |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |

| `swiss-neuchatel-castle-lake-*` | [Panorama of Neuchâtel and its castle at sunrise](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Panorama_Neuch%C3%A2tel_et_le_ch%C3%A2teau_au_lever_du_soleil.jpg) | Martouf | [CC0 1.0](https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/) | Cropped to 2:1; transformed to credible sunny weather using a reference-constrained sky and lighting edit; the lower city and rooftops were recomposited from the source photograph and the landmark composition was checked against it; resized, metadata stripped and encoded as AVIF/WebP. |

The Aarau photograph shows the real old town, Stadtkirche and Obertorturm. The Neuchâtel
photograph shows the real old town, Château de Neuchâtel, Collégiale and Lake Neuchâtel.
